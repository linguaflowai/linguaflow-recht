## 2. Core Principle

LinguaFlow Live Translator does not operate its own server. As the developer, I do not receive any translation texts, inputs, voice recordings, or API keys. All data generated in the app remains locally on your device.

## 3. Minimum Age

The app is intended only for individuals aged 18 and older. The AI providers whose services you use with your own key require this minimum age.

## 4. Data Stored Only on Your Device

- Your API keys (stored encrypted locally on your device, excluded from Android cloud backup)
- Selected languages (Person A & B), voice output settings, and appearance (Dark, Light, System)
- Translation history and favorites (up to 200 entries)
- Pro unlock status and daily free usage counter (50 free voice translations per day)

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.
