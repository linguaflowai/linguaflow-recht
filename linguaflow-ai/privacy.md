## 2. Basic principle

LinguaFlow AI does not operate its own server. As the developer, I receive no learning data, no inputs, no voice recordings and no API keys. All data created in the app is stored on your device.

## 3. Minimum age

The app is intended only for people aged 18 or over. The AI providers whose services you use with your own key require this minimum age.

## 4. Data stored only on your device

- Your API key (stored locally on your device only, not included in the Android backup)
- Selected learning language, level, topic, voice and appearance (dark, light, system)
- Learning progress, flashcards, weekly overview
- Reward data (points, levels, missions, journey, souvenirs, pet)

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

## 5. Transmission to AI services

For exercises, translations, country information and the pronunciation tutor, the app sends requests directly from your device, using your own key, to:

- Google (Gemini API), main provider
- Groq, fallback in case of country restriction, daily limit or error (text only, not voice recordings)

The data sent consists of the request text (for example language, level, topic and country) and technical connection data such as your IP address.

**Pronunciation tutor:** If you use the pronunciation tutor, the app records your voice with the microphone after your confirmation (12 seconds at most) and sends this recording together with the practice sentence to Google (Gemini API) so that the AI can produce an assessment of your pronunciation. The recording is not sent to Groq. Without a Google key, the pronunciation tutor cannot be used. The assessment is made by AI and may be inaccurate. The app does not store the recording for later use and deletes the temporary file on your device after the evaluation. If a recording is cancelled early, the temporary file may remain in the app's private cache until the system clears it, you clear the app data or you uninstall the app.

The privacy policies and terms of use of the respective provider apply. These providers may process data outside the EU, including in the USA.

**Important regarding Google:** If you use a free Gemini key, your inputs, your voice recordings and the responses may, under Google's terms, be used to improve its products and be read by humans. Different rules apply to a paid key. Groq also processes requests under its own retention rules; you can find details in the provider's documentation.

Therefore, please do not record anything private in voice recordings and do not disclose personal or confidential information.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (provision of the function you have chosen).

## 6. Purchases via Google Play

Unlocking further languages is handled through Google Play Billing. Payment data is processed solely by Google. I do not receive any payment data, only the confirmation that a purchase has been made.

## 7. Android backup

If automatic Android backup is active, settings and learning progress may be backed up via your Google account. The API key is not backed up.

## 8. No advertising, no tracking

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking services. Google provides me, as the developer, with aggregated statistics through the Play Console (for example installations and crash rates). Google collects these itself; I receive no information about individual people from them.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since I do not store any personal data about you myself, these rights relate mainly to the providers named above.

## 10. Changes

This policy may be updated if the app changes. The version most recently published applies.
